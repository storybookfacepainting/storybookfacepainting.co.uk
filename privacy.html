Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Emma’s Storybook Face Painting collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or book face-painting services. We handle personal information under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible for your information?

Emma Collins, trading as Emma’s Storybook Face Painting, is the data controller. For privacy questions or requests, contact Emma at emma@storybookfacepainting.co.uk or call 07792 628692.

What information we collect

When you make an enquiry or booking, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, event date and details, venue/access information, and messages you choose to send. Required form fields are marked; if you do not provide them, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry.

For safety, you or a parent/guardian may tell us about an attendee’s allergies, sensitive skin, eczema, or other relevant health conditions. These details are health information and receive extra protection. We will only take or publish photographs of people with their prior permission, or the prior permission of a parent/guardian for a child. Agreeing to photographs is optional and is not a condition of booking.

Our website host and form-delivery provider may process technical information, such as information needed to deliver, secure, and prevent abuse of the website or form.

How and why we use information

  • Enquiries and bookings: to reply, provide quotes, arrange and deliver a booking, and handle payment or cancellation details. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before a contract and performing the contract.
  • Accounts and legal records: to meet tax, accounting, insurance, or other legal requirements. Our lawful basis is compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Safety information: to assess whether face painting is suitable and adapt or refuse a design where needed. Where health information is provided, Emma will seek and record explicit consent for this specific safety purpose before using it. You may withdraw that consent; without necessary safety information or consent, a service may not be possible.
  • Photographs: to show examples of Emma’s work in a portfolio or marketing, including this website and social media, only after the photographed adult or a child’s parent/guardian has given prior permission. Permission is optional and separate from booking. You can withdraw permission for future use or request removal by contacting Emma.
  • Protecting the business and resolving issues: to keep appropriate records, respond to complaints, and establish or defend legal claims. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in running and protecting the business, balanced against your rights.

We do not use enquiry details for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not send marketing emails from the enquiry form.

Who we share information with

Enquiry form details are sent through FormSubmit, which processes and forwards submissions to Emma by email. Emma’s email provider then handles the message. The site is hosted using GitHub Pages, which may process technical data when delivering the site. These providers handle information under their own service terms and privacy information. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Some service providers may process technical or form information outside the UK. If you would like details of any international transfers and the safeguards used, contact Emma using the details above. When you follow a link to Instagram, Instagram’s own privacy terms apply.

How long we keep information

We aim to apply these retention periods:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: delete within 12 months of the last contact, unless a complaint, dispute, or legal claim means relevant information must be kept longer.
  • Event, contact, and safety details: delete within 12 months after the event, or sooner once no longer needed. Health details will be kept only for as long as needed to assess and deliver the service safely, and then deleted as soon as practicable. Relevant records may be retained longer if there is an incident, complaint, dispute, or legal claim.
  • Invoices and tax/accounting records: keep for the period required by the tax and company-record rules that apply to Emma’s business. For example, HMRC generally requires self-employed business records to be kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January filing deadline for the relevant tax year. Different periods may apply to a limited company.
  • Photographs: retain only while permission remains in place and the images are useful for the portfolio or marketing. If permission is withdrawn, Emma will stop future use and remove images from channels she controls within a reasonable time.

These periods are reviewed periodically. Information may be kept longer where needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, and is securely deleted when there is no longer a valid reason to retain it.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of use, or a copy in a portable format. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent; this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

Right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting Emma. You can also object to direct marketing at any time.

Email emma@storybookfacepainting.co.uk to make a request. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions.

Cookies and website links

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The enquiry form is delivered by FormSubmit, which may use its own technical cookies or similar technologies when you use the form. Instagram and other external services may also use their own technologies when you visit them. Please check those providers’ privacy information for details.

Questions or complaints

Please contact Emma first if you have a concern about how your information is handled. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or by calling 0303 123 1113.

We may update this policy if our services or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
