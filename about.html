About Me

I’m Emma, the face behind Emma’s Storybook Facepainting!

I’m a busy mum of two who loves to get creative and always enjoy activities I can share with my children, especially when it adds a bit of magic and fun.

My facepainting journey started during maternity leave, when I combined facepainting with favourite films and storybooks (hence the name!). I love bringing children’s imagination to life, whether it’s a magical unicorn, fiery dragon, fairy princess or stomping dinosaur.

I also love to create intricate floral or vivid pattern designs for adults too, perfect for festivals, special occasions and expressing your own style.

Through Emma’s Storybook Facepainting, I hope to add a bit of excitement and fun to special occasions and parties.
